# brand-admin

Phase 7. Every route here is mounted behind `requireAuth, requireBrandScope` (Phase 3) and every service function double-checks the resolved document's `brand` against `req.user.brand` before returning it — cross-tenant isolation is a correctness-critical requirement, verified by a dedicated Jest/E2E suite (two real brands, cross-access always 403/empty), not just assumed from the middleware alone.

Modules: `brand-crm/brandDashboard` (live unified-ticket cohorts, KPI comparisons and permission-trimmed billing at `/brand/dashboard`), `brand-crm/brandReports` (eight ticket/billing reports, filtered CSV/XLSX streams at `/brand/reports/:report`; retires the old `/brand/reports` SR reports), `brand-crm/billing` (transactional NCC-to-brand invoices and receipts; `/brand/billing`; replaces retired manual `/brand/invoices`), `brand-crm/brandRate` (dated geographic rates; `/brand/rates`, separate NCC payouts and completion charge snapshots), `replacementApproval` + `reverseLogisticsReturn` (both also serve as their own creation entry point — no serviceProvider-side trigger exists yet), `brandCatalog` (3-level `MasterService` → `SubBrand` → `BrandProduct` hierarchy), `team` (+ add/remove members), `brandRole` + `brandUser` (brand-scoped RBAC — `BrandUser` folded into the shared `User` collection, same Phase 1 deviation as everywhere else), `generatedDocument` (PDF rendering itself out of scope, just tracks the record).

This phase also closed a gap left open since Phase 4: `service-requests/serviceRequest.routes.js`'s list/get/status-transition routes now properly scope `brand_admin` by `req.user.brand` (previously any brand_admin could see and transition *any* service request). See BACKEND_CONTEXT.md §5 and `../../../docs/DATA_MODEL.md`'s Phase 7 addendum.


Brand CRM Phase 16 adds `/brand/audit-logs` (`audit:view`) with actor snapshots, diffs, tenant-scoped search and CSV export. `brand-crm/auditCoverage.js` inventories every mutating Brand Panel rule; `auditMutations.js` supplies explicit adapters for legacy writers plus a request-local safety net. See `docs/brand-crm/phase-16-audit-log.md` for coverage and legacy/transaction boundaries. Ticket audit actions are normalized separately from stable timeline events.
